Run: Bioretention_truth_drift_SE / Bioretention_assimilation_MCMC_drift_SE, started 2026-08-11. Cycles 1–— of ~243; the run is still advancing. Truth and assimilation models differ in soil discretisation (10 layers vs 4), in the moisture-probe footprint, and in ERA5 forcing cell — the injected structural error.

One artifact is visible in Figures 1, 3, 4 and 5 as a short backward step near simulation day 44043: at cycle 71 the calibration window was placed at 43925–44025 instead of 43943–44043, so the x-axis doubles back by 18 days for a single cycle before jumping forward by 25. The window end is taken from the newest buffered observation, and the observation buffer is replaced on every refresh rather than appended to; the truth server was rewriting its output file when that fetch landed, so the buffer came back short. It affected 1 of 216 cycles and self-corrected on the next refresh.
